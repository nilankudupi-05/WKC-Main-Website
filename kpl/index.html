The Journey

How It Works

From registration to grand finale - a step-by-step journey to the national stage.

01
Register & Choose Your Category

Register and pick the category your child loves.

02
Level 1 & Level 2 - Online Rounds

Every child starts at Level 1 and moves to Level 2 only after qualifying. Submit entries online - photos, video or writing depending on category. Expert judges score every entry.

03
Semi-Finals

Top performers from Level 2 qualify for the Semi-Finals.

04
Grand Finale

The national final. January–February. The country's best young talent compete for the Kids Premier League title and a magazine feature.
