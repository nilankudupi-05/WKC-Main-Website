Why Little Champions League

How the Little Champions League Works

Three simple steps - from home to celebrated champion.

01

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What parents ask about the Little Champions League.

No. It is participation-first. There are no rankings or elimination rounds. Every child who submits receives a certificate and personalised feedback. The goal is to celebrate effort and build early confidence.
The Little Champions League (LCL) is the Junior division, designed for children aged 3–5. For children aged 6 and above, the Senior division - the Kids Premier League (KPL) - runs competitively across four categories.
The Junior division runs two categories: Build It (LEGO building) and Color Wizards (drawing and painting). Handwriting Champs and Master Orator are Senior-only, since they ask for skills that develop after age 5.
Not at all. Submissions can be photographed at home in minutes. No special materials, sets or preparation required. If your child builds something out of bricks or draws a picture - that's the entry.
All entries are reviewed by WKC's early childhood educators using age-appropriate rubrics focused on expression, effort and engagement - not technical skill. Our judges are trained to look for joy and confidence, not perfection.
Every child receives a digital participation certificate with their name, category and a personalised note from the judge. Top entries may receive physical certificates, medals and potential magazine features.
